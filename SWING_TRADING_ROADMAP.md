## 🎯 **SUCCESS METRICS FOR SWING TRADERS**

### **Risk Management**
- ✅ **Max 2% risk per trade**
- ✅ **Max 6-8% total portfolio risk**
- ✅ **Stop losses on every position**
- ✅ **Position sizing based on Kelly Criterion**

### **Entry Criteria**
- ✅ **Multi-timeframe alignment (75%+)**
- ✅ **Confirmed swing pattern**
- ✅ **Volume confirmation**
- ✅ **Clear risk/reward ratio (minimum 2:1)**

### **Exit Management**
- ✅ **Partial profit taking at targets**
- ✅ **Trailing stops for runners**
- ✅ **Time-based exits (max 21 days)**
- ✅ **Pre-earnings position adjustments**

### **Performance Targets**
- 🎯 **Win Rate: 50-60%**
- 🎯 **Average R:R: 2.5:1**
- 🎯 **Monthly Return: 3-8%**
- 🎯 **Max Drawdown: <15%**
